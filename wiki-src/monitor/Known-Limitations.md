# Known Limitations

## Operator Coverage

- Currently integrates telemetry from TAG and NTS. TBG (Transportadora Brasileira Gasoduto Bolívia-Brasil) and local distribution companies (LDCs) are not yet integrated into the automated line pack monitor.

## Telemetry Timing

- Snapshots reflect upstream publication schedules. NTS updates hourly; TAG updates upon periodic telemetry batch releases. Minor reporting lags may occur during upstream SCADA maintenance windows.
