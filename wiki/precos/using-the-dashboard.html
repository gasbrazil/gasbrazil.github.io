Using the Dashboard

ANP Prices tracks natural-gas price disclosures under ANP Resolution 52/2011 — monthly R$/MMBtu series with volumes, across three separate markets published as three separate ANP files.

KPI row

Headline figure: the latest Santos basin producer price.

Three charts, three markets

  • Producer sales by basin — Santos, Campos, and an aggregated "Other basins" series. Toggle any combination on/off via the series picker above the chart.
  • Sales to distributors & free consumers — split two ways at once: thermal vs. non-thermal, and by region (Norte-Nordeste, Sudeste-Sul-Centro-Oeste groupings vary by market type — see the picker for the exact five series).
  • Sales to marketers — a single national monthly series; no picker, since there's only the one series to show.

Each chart has its own legend showing exactly which series are currently on.

Table and exports

The table lists the full monthly series across all three markets. Download CSV and Export Excel follow it.

Reading a gap

A blank cell for a given month/series is ANP's own confidentiality suppression, not a zero and not a missing fetch — see Known Limitations.
